Investors

The trust layer for private AI.

Satoris earns a take rate on every private inference request, settled in USDC, on a network of staked confidential GPUs.

Business model

A take rate on private inference.

Usage fees

Every request settles in USDC. Satoris keeps the spread between the catalog price and the provider payout, visible in each receipt.

Provider stake

GPU operators stake USDC to receive traffic. Stake backs the network's guarantees without a speculative token.

Private deployments

Teams with regulated data can pin requests to their own attested nodes and keep verifiable receipts for audits.

Why Satoris wins

Built for the buyers who can't use AI today.

Regulated teams

Health, legal and finance teams get a privacy guarantee they can verify and an audit trail on-chain, instead of a vendor's promise.

Autonomous agents

Agents pay per call from a scoped wallet with spend limits. No signup, no API key to leak, no invoice.

GPU providers

Operators with confidential GPUs earn USDC for capacity that buyers can trust, backed by their stake.
